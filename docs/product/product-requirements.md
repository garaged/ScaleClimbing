# ScaleClimbing Product Requirements

## Vision

ScaleClimbing helps musicians recognize notes, intervals, chords, and scales visually and by ear through short game-like practice loops.

## MVP objective

Prove a fun iOS-first vertical slice around notes and major/minor triads before expanding into microphone recognition, MIDI, staff notation, guitar, or desktop editions.

## Primary users

- Beginners learning note names and sound relationships.
- Self-taught musicians who know shapes but lack theory fluency.
- Intermediate players strengthening ear, chord, and scale recognition.

## MVP capabilities

- Visual note recognition.
- Scale and chord construction.
- Generated ear-training prompts.
- Deterministic answer evaluation.
- Offline-first local usage.
- Adaptive progression design prepared by specs, but not fully implemented in M0.

## Non-goals for M0

- Accounts or cloud sync.
- Microphone capture or chord transcription.
- Licensed song catalog.
- Purchases or subscriptions.
- Social multiplayer.
- Windows/Linux desktop clients.
