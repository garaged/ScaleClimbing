# M12 Notation Trail — short acceptance

1. Open **Notation Trail** on an iPhone simulator. Confirm it has one navigation title, default **Treble clef · F#4**, a readable five-line staff with a sharp sign, and audible **Hear note** playback.
2. In Explore, confirm the written note explanation, enharmonic C#/Db context and C/Cm/C7 chord-symbol examples are legible. Change to Bass clef and confirm the staff position changes.
3. In Read, confirm the prompt does not disclose F#4 before answering. Choose **F#**, confirm success and an explanation of its clef position.
4. In Dictate, use **Hear melody**. Submit Do–Mi and confirm retry feedback; clear, submit Do–Re–Mi and confirm success.
5. Confirm VoiceOver describes staff positions clearly, all controls fit an iPhone simulator, and no microphone or network permission appears.
