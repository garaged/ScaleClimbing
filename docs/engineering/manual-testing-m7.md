# M7 Chord Atlas — short acceptance

1. On the M7 branch, open `Apps/iOS/ScaleClimbing.xcodeproj` on an iPhone 17 simulator; open **Chord Atlas** (Harmony Heights).
2. In **Listen**, tap **Hear chord** on the first C major round. The quality must remain undisclosed until you answer. Tap **Major Triad**; feedback should report correct, C–E–G and semitones 0,4,7; advance to **Minor Triad**. Score should persist.
3. Switch **Chord family** to **Seventh Chords**. Confirm only five seventh qualities are offered and a four-note reference sounds. On C Dominant Seventh, choose Dominant Seventh and verify the explanation includes semitones 0,4,7,10.
4. Switch to **Build** and select the C major seventh notes C–E–G–B on the piano after changing family/round as needed. Deliberately omit a note, **Check chord**, hear the selected notes, then replay and correct. Confirm score and retry behavior; **Clear** resets replay.
5. Verify responsive buttons, horizontal keyboard scrolling and a quick VoiceOver pass. No microphone permissions, accounts or network required.

Swift/domain and iPhone UI acceptance cover catalog structure, blind disclosure, score locking, construction, replay and route regression. Human manual validation focuses on perception and ergonomics.
