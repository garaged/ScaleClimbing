# M14 Visual Design System — short acceptance

1. On an iPhone simulator, launch ScaleClimbing. Confirm Home has one **ScaleClimbing** navigation title, the “Learn music by doing” introduction, one prominent **Today’s Practice** Learning Path card, and grouped practice cards. Every prior practice area must remain discoverable.
2. Switch between light and dark appearance. Confirm the indigo/surface system remains legible, cards separate clearly from the canvas, and no status relies on color alone.
3. Open at least one practice area from each zone. Confirm each has one navigation title, the same calm canvas/spacing, a clearly dominant primary action, rounded feedback surface, and familiar button hierarchy while retaining its original learning controls.
4. In **Ear Training**, play, answer, and advance. In **Scale Explorer** or **Rhythm Trail**, switch modes, make an incorrect attempt, replay/hear audio, correct it, and advance. Confirm audio and scoring behavior is unchanged.
5. With Larger Text and VoiceOver enabled, verify Home cards, prompt, feedback, keyboard/choice controls and Next/Retry actions remain readable, reachable, and named. Confirm no microphone, network, notification, or account permission is requested.
