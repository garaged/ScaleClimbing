# M13 Learning Path — short acceptance

1. On the simulator home screen, confirm the ScaleClimbing icon has a level white note head, vertical stem and curved flag above the colored steps; it must not appear skewed.
2. Open **Learning Path** on an iPhone simulator. Confirm there is one navigation title, **Today’s mixed practice**, and up to three clearly separated practice cards.
3. Confirm each card explains why it is present, offers an **Open …** link to its existing practice area, and a prominent **Mark reviewed** control. Opening a route must not change its mastery by itself.
4. Mark one card reviewed. Confirm the queue refreshes and the reviewed item is scheduled later rather than silently counted again.
5. Enable **Simplified explanations** and confirm cards change to the concise “Review due.” explanation. Relaunch the app and confirm the choice persists.
6. Toggle **Large controls** off/on and confirm the review button visibly changes size, controls remain usable on an iPhone, VoiceOver names the controls, and no account, notification, microphone, or network permission is requested.
