# M11 Timbre Lab — short acceptance

1. Open **Timbre Lab** on an iPhone simulator. Confirm there is one navigation title, the default is **A3 · Bright spectrum**, and **Hear reference** is clearly audible.
2. In Explore, hear Pure, Warm and Bright. Confirm the A3 fundamental stays named while their partial recipes differ. Hear the octave, equal fifth, just fifth and beating examples.
3. In Listen, confirm the profile identity is hidden until an answer. Answer Bright correctly and confirm the explanation names its upper partials.
4. In Build, submit the default partial 1-only recipe for Bright and receive retry feedback. Add 2 through 6, use **Hear attempt**, then confirm the corrected recipe succeeds.
5. Confirm VoiceOver labels are understandable, controls remain usable on an iPhone-sized simulator, and no microphone or network permission appears.
