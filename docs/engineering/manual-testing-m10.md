# M10 Rhythm Trail — short acceptance

1. Open **Rhythm Trail** on an iPhone simulator. Confirm there is one navigation title only, default **4/4** is shown, and Explore plays audible accented pulses.
2. Confirm **Tempo** defaults to **108 BPM — Comfortable**. Play 100 and 120 BPM; 100 must be visibly/audibly slower, while 120 is quicker but still comfortable.
3. In Listen, play and identify 2/4, 3/4, 4/4 and 6/8. Confirm the identity stays hidden until answered.
4. In Tap, submit three taps in 4/4, receive retry feedback, then complete four. Switch to 6/8 and confirm it describes two compound 3+3 pulses.
5. Confirm VoiceOver labels are clear and no microphone or network permission appears.
