# M10 Rhythm Trail — short acceptance

1. Open **Rhythm Trail** on an iPhone simulator. In Explore, verify 4/4 shows four accented simple-beat pulses and hear the click pattern.
2. In Listen, play each meter and identify 2/4, 3/4, 4/4 and 6/8. Confirm identity stays hidden until answered.
3. In Tap, tap one pulse short in 4/4, check and retry with four taps. Switch to 6/8 and confirm it describes two compound 3+3 pulses.
4. Confirm quick VoiceOver labels and that no microphone/network permission appears.
