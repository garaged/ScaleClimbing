# M9 Harmony Path — short acceptance

1. Open the M9 branch in an iPhone 17 simulator and open **Harmony Path** (Harmony Heights).
2. In **Explore**, verify C major lists I C major, ii D minor, IV F major and V G major with tonic/predominant/dominant labels. **Hear cadence** should clearly play G major to C major for the authentic V–I cadence.
3. Switch to **Listen**. The cadence identity must stay hidden until an answer. Choose **Authentic cadence** and verify it scores once, reveals V–I and explains dominant resolving to tonic.
4. Switch to **Build**. Enter I–V for the authentic cadence, check, hear/replay the attempt and confirm correction feedback. Clear, enter V–I, and verify correct feedback.
5. Change the key to D major and hear the authentic cadence again: it should move A major to D major. Confirm controls remain legible and complete a brief VoiceOver pass.

All audio remains generated locally; no account, network, microphone permission or third-party asset is expected.
